# Contributing

This repository accepts papers on LLMs for:

- Politics
- Geopolitics
- Policymaking
- Strategic studies
- Decision-making

Please include:

- Title
- Year
- Paper URL
- Category
- One short note explaining why the paper belongs in this list

Do not submit finance-only, trading, stock-prediction, portfolio-management, or generic financial LLM papers. They are outside the scope unless the paper is directly about policymaking, geopolitics, or strategic decision-making.
