# Contributing

Please include the following fields for every paper submission:

- Title
- Year
- Paper URL
- Category
- Short note on why it belongs in this list
- Optional: code, dataset, model, benchmark, or project links

High-signal additions should be directly relevant to LLMs, foundation models,
retrieval, reasoning, agents, forecasting, simulation, or language-centric
benchmarks in strategic domains such as finance, markets, geoeconomics,
geopolitics, governance, policy, diplomacy, wargaming, or multi-agent social
simulation.

Generic ML/NLP papers should only be included when they are repeated foundation
references across the LLM-for-strategies literature.
